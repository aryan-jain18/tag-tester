Built for the real world

The details other auditors miss.

  • Consent-aware: flags any tag that fires before gtag('consent', 'default') resolves, or that ignores a denied region.
  • Vendor-context PII: short aliases like em, ph, fn, ln are only treated as identifiers when the receiving vendor actually uses them that way.
  • Survives service-worker suspensions: MV3-correct state hydration means you won't lose your audit after a few idle minutes.
  • First-party cookie awareness: detects CNAME-cloaked tracking and server-side GTM endpoints on your own subdomains.

Frequently asked

Does Tag Tester send my data anywhere?

No. Every audit runs locally inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to Kaliper or any third party.

Will it work on staging / password-protected sites?

Yes — as long as you can open the site in Chrome and the DevTools panel attaches, Tag Tester can audit it.

How is this different from GTM Preview or Omnibug?

GTM Preview only shows what GTM fired. Omnibug shows requests. Tag Tester combines both with consent reasoning, PII detection, and plain-English summaries aimed at non-engineers.

Can I export the results?

Yes. The toolbar has an Export JSON button with the full audit — findings, requests, cookies, dataLayer and page state.

What about Safari or Firefox?

Chrome & Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc) are supported today. Firefox support is on the roadmap.

Is there a paid version?

Not yet. The extension is free during beta; a team tier with shared audit history is in design.
